OLISTUDIOS PROJECT 02

OliFlight

A continuously developing browser-based spaceflight simulation.

THE PROJECT

Build. Launch. Explore.

OliFlight is a sandbox spaceflight simulation built around experimentation and exploration.

The project is designed to grow continuously, beginning with spacecraft and expanding over time toward a broader spaceflight experience.

PLATFORM

Browser

Designed to be accessible directly from the web.

STYLE

Sandbox

Experiment with spacecraft and different flight possibilities.

DEVELOPMENT

Continuous

A project intended to evolve through regular development.
