Vanguard:
Frozen Front

A fictional Cold War tactical FPS set in the mid-1980s.

THE GAME

A different kind of Cold War.

Vanguard: Frozen Front is a free browser-based 3D FPS combining close-quarters tactical combat with larger-scale military operations.

Players take control of Vanguard, a specialized U.S. reconnaissance and special-operations agency formed to respond to escalating Eastern Bloc threats. The campaign moves from covert operations and urban engagements to large conventional battles across Northern and Central Europe.

SETTING

Mid-1980s

A fictional Cold War conflict across NATO and Eastern Bloc territory.

FORMAT

Browser FPS

Designed around accessible performance and low-poly visuals.

CAMPAIGN

15 Missions

A campaign spanning covert operations, Arctic warfare, and major battles.

DEVELOPMENT

Built to expand.

Frozen Front is being developed as an evolving project. New missions, systems, environments, and features can be introduced through future updates.
